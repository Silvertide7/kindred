## 1.4.1
---
- Auto-Mount no longer applies while you're in water, so you aren't mounted and immediately thrown off (or given an underwater horse when water summons are off). The pet summons normally instead
- Auto-Mount now respects mods that restrict who can ride their mounts
- Horses summoned mid-eat or mid-rear no longer keep doing it once you're riding
- Auto-Mount's headroom check now uses the pet's actual size, so resized mobs don't clip into the ceiling
- Auto-Mount no longer places your pet next to lava, and no longer triggers mid-jump (the pet would spawn in mid-air)
- If Auto-Mount can't mount you (for example, summoning right after getting off another mount), the pet now moves to a free spot beside you instead of standing inside you
